### AI University Games Hackathon (Prosus x AISO)

1. **Lekkerly**
* **Idea:** Personal AI-powered nutrition companion
* **Features:** Object recognition, nutrient tracking, AI recipe transformation for custom diets, Albert Heijn shopping list generation, one-click grocery ordering
* **Effect & Goal:** Connect food inspiration to execution from photo to plate, making healthy eating effortless and efficient


2. **PillPal**
* **Idea:** Medication safety and management platform for older adults and families
* **Features:** Prescription tracking, automated dosage reminders, automatic supply refills, calendar sync for interaction detection, integrated chatbot
* **Effect & Goal:** Prevent harmful drug interactions and remove the daily stress of complex medication routines


3. **StudyBuddy**
* **Idea:** Academic management and study assistant
* **Features:** Integration with Canvas and BrightSpace, automated syllabus and course extraction, practice exam generation, personalized study schedules, group session calendar sync
* **Effect & Goal:** Reduce academic disorganization and keep university students structured and prepared

### TechJam 2026 (Conversational E-Commerce Search Challenge)

1. **SATU (Shopping Assistant That Understands)**
* **Idea:** Zero-dependency, ultra-low-latency conversational shopping copilot
* **Features:** 10-turn clarifying dialogue, top-10 candidate slate ranking over a 50,000-item apparel catalog, 100% Python standard library implementation without external packages, network calls, or LLM tokens (p50 latency under 1 ms)
* **Effect & Goal:** Accurately surface targeted products from natural-language descriptions without cloud compute overhead or API costs
